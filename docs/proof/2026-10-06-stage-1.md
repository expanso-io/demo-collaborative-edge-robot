# Stage 1 end-to-end run, 2026-10-06

The whole demo ran on one Mac from `scripts/run`: six Expanso Edge nodes on
`127.0.0.1`, the presenter page on port 4180, and every message on the bus.
Each beat below was driven in a real browser. The decisions shown are the ones
the coordinator pipeline published on the bus.

A stop and a go sent together left everything paused with the reason "stop
wins within 1.5 seconds", and the destination stayed selected.

## The real camera

In a second run, the browser's camera was fed a video of the printed card 1
held in the middle of a 640 by 480 frame. The camera device recognised it at
100% confidence, and the coordinator selected Station 1
([7-live-camera.png](stage-1/7-live-camera.png)).

Each camera message was 189 bytes and reported `raw_bytes` of 921,600, the size
of the frame it read. The frame never left the browser, so each recognition
replaced 4,876 times its own size in raw video.

## Not yet verified

- **The live microphone.** The go and stop model recognised 12 of 12 test
  recordings through its own test page, `node tests/audio/verify.mjs`. In this
  run the headless browser's fake microphone delivered no sound, so the live
  path from a real microphone to the bus was not exercised. Saying "go" and
  "stop" into the Mac's microphone at the first rehearsal closes this.
- **The physical camera.** The card was read from a recorded feed, not the
  Mac's own webcam in stage lighting. The confidence threshold should be
  checked at the first rehearsal with the printed cards.
